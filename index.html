Our Core Offerings

Professional Lawn & Yard Care For Every Property

From weekly mowing to complete overgrown yard restorations, Carlos and the J.Jc DFW team treat every lawn like our own.

Lawn Mowing & Precision Edging

Consistent, razor-sharp cuts with precision string trimming around fences, flower beds, walkways, and driveways, followed by thorough blow-off clean-up.

  • Weekly & Bi-Weekly schedules
  • Sharp blades prevent turf damage

Complete Yard Transformations

Have an overgrown lawn or rental property that needs rescue? We bring out heavy-duty equipment to restore unruly yards back to healthy, manicured condition.

  • Tall weed & brush clearing
  • Dramatic same-day transformations

Shrub & Hedge Trimming

Neatly sculpted hedges, bushes, and decorative plants. Proper pruning techniques to promote healthy foliage growth and curb appeal.

  • Symmetrical shaping & deadwood removal
  • Full clippings haul-away

Seasonal Clean-Up & Leaf Removal

Spring refreshing and fall leaf cleanup. We clear suffocating wet leaves, twigs, debris, and fallen branches to protect your turf health.

  • Gutter and bed clearing
  • Yard waste disposal included

Fresh hardwood mulch delivery and spread to retain soil moisture, prevent weed growth, and give your garden beds a sharp, designer finish.

  • Black, brown, and natural cedar mulch
  • Bed re-edging and weed barrier prep
